\section{Calibration, implementation error, and the evidence boundary}\label{sec:deploymentcalibration}
The deployment interface is the one stated and proved in
\citet[Section~39]{NoguerAlonso2026Execution}. With a common initial law, observation class, feasible masks and terminal
reward error at most $\eta_N$, suppose the fitted rewards and next-state
kernels satisfy $\lvert r_k-\widehat r_k\rvert\le\eta_k$ and
$\mathrm{TV}(P_k^a,\widehat P_k^a)\le\epsilon_k$ uniformly over feasible states
and messages, and $S_k$ bounds the span of fitted continuation values. Then, with
$B_N=\eta_N$ and $B_k=B_{k+1}+\eta_k+\epsilon_kS_{k+1}$ every common feasible
policy has $\lvert J-\widehat J\rvert\le B_0$, and a compiled policy with fitted
action gaps $\zeta_k$ loses at most $2B_0+\widehat{\mathbb E}\sum_k\zeta_k$
\citep[Theorem~39.1]{NoguerAlonso2026Execution}. For prespecified state--action rows with
independent draws, simultaneous radii follow from
\citet[Proposition~39.2]{NoguerAlonso2026Execution}.

Here the reward includes the marked fill charge \eqref{eq:jointmarkedcharge}, and the kernel includes
tagged-rank transitions and pending-cancel states. Theorems~\ref{thm:intervalcontrol} and~\ref{thm:baselinequoting} are the
discounted analogues and use value enclosures over a parameter box $\Theta$
rather than per-row radii. The row radii can populate $\Theta$ only if the rows
are the state--message pairs of the finite queue model and the independent
sampling assumption holds; a single dependent message stream does not satisfy
it without a martingale, mixing or block argument. Replay and off-policy
evaluation need the support, invariance and unconfoundedness conditions of
\citet[Section~39.4]{NoguerAlonso2026Execution}; Proposition~\ref{prop:coverage} is an instance of what
fails without action coverage. Message conformance, measured latency, fee tiers
and incident recovery remain external evidence requirements.
